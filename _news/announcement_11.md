Two papers accepted at CVPR 2026 (one in main track and one in findings). Congratulations to Aishwarya for the amazing work.  [CCI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.12978) presents striking visualization results for CLIP, while [Lite-embed](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.09661) enables adapting CLIP to rare classes with just a few images, without modifying the model. Wonderful collaboration continues with Aishwarya and Srikrishna.
