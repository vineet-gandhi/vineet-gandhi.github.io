Our paper called [CLARIS: Clear and Intelligible Speech from Whispered and Dysarthric Voices](https://vineet-gandhi.github.io/assets/pdf/Whisper_to_Speech_CHI_2026.pdf) accepted at CHI 2026. This breakthrough work shows that disarthric speech can be real-time converted into normal speech. Congratulations to Neil, Yash, and Shirish, with special mention to Yash on his first PhD paper. Speech samples available [here:](https://claris-w2s.github.io/CLARIS/).
